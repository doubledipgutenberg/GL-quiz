# Illustrations

Bold single-pen line drawings (3.6px strokes on the 800px scenes, 3.6–4.2px on the 240px spots, details ~2.3px) on cream fills, with **lamp-300 (#F6D27A)** for lit windows, bulbs and clock faces. The pen is the course's:

- **Paris / French** — ink **fr-pen #1F45A8**, water **fr-tint #DCE4F5**: `paris-scene`, `eiffel-tower`, `cafe-table`, `croissant`, `coffee-cup`, `awning`.
- **London / English** — ink **en-pen #BA2820**, water **en-tint #F6DCD6**: `london-scene`, `big-ben`, `double-decker`, `phone-box`, `teacup`, `umbrella`.

Always on the `plate` colour (cream, in the Night theme too). Scenes (800×520) go on course cards; spots (240×240) on vocab cards and empty states. Never mix the two cities in one course.

New drawings: round caps, flat fills only (pen, cream, lamp, tint), no gradients, no second line colour.
