# Stickers

32 feedback stickers: a cast of little textured characters (egg, box, ball, bean and dome bodies with a hair tuft), drawn in the course colour. Body: grainy fill in the mid tone (#93ACEB Paris, #EF9D92 London). Lines, legs and arms: the course pen (**fr-pen #1F45A8**, **en-pen #BA2820**) with cream fills. Sparkles, stars and props: **lamp-300 #F6D27A**. No frame, a soft ground shadow. 220×226.

- `richtig-<course>-<pose>.svg` (20): cheer with beret / bowler, thumbs up, star eyes, croissant held high, medal, clap, heart eyes, crown, jump (baguette / umbrella), flex, kiss, tea toast, rocket.
- `nicht-ganz-<course>-<pose>.svg` (12): shrug, oops, thinking (with croissant / teacup), head-scratch with tongue out, reaching for a star, light-bulb moment, peeking through hands, open-arms hug.
- `stickers.json` — exclamation and German line for each; rotation rules on the FeedbackSticker card.

Never sad, never red-for-wrong: the *Nicht ganz* characters are on the learner's side.
